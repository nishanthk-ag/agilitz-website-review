Our story

Built by BizApps veterans, one delivery centre at a time.

A timeline of how Agilitz has grown from a Microsoft Business Applications specialist into a global delivery organisation with products, partnerships and customer programmes across regions.

2020
2026
2020

Agilitz is founded

Agilitz is founded by a team already 15–20+ years deep in Microsoft Business Applications, with a mission to build a valued global company that helps enterprises gain a competitive edge through Microsoft platform expertise.

2021

Expansion across Europe and the Middle East

Early Dynamics 365 Supply Chain, ERP and Customer Engagement engagements expand into a broader customer base across the UK, Europe and the Middle East.

2022

Partner ecosystem and US presence

Agilitz partners with Avalara for D365-integrated tax compliance and establishes a US presence, extending customer coverage across Europe, the Americas and Asia.

2023

Leadership, partnerships and Bangalore delivery centre

Agilitz expands its partner ecosystem, is recognised as a Microsoft ISV Success partner, strengthens the leadership team and launches its Bangalore global delivery centre.

2024

Litz Suite and Bhubaneswar expansion

Litz Suite products — DocuLitz, ImageLitz and LitzFile Sync — expand the Microsoft Business Applications proposition, while a second India delivery centre opens in Bhubaneswar.

2026

One global delivery model

Agilitz operates across the UK, USA and India, combining customer-facing teams with scalable delivery capability for Microsoft Business Applications, Data & AI and managed services.
