Guided discovery

Assess current Salesforce modules, customisations and integrations through a structured discovery process.

Migration readiness

Ready reports

Combined expertise

AI-ready destination

Migration lifecycle

Consult. Migrate. Operate.

The attached Agilitz migration approach uses LitzSF to accelerate assessment, migration and post-migration optimisation.

Consult

Assess the Salesforce estate

Review implemented Salesforce modules and quantify customisations and integrations currently in place.

Migrate

Move to Dynamics 365 CE

Use LitzSF to support migration, complete required data movement and finish ecosystem integrations or configurations.

Operate

Optimise after migration

Optimise Dynamics 365 CE, check performance, extend with Power Platform and Copilot, and provide hypercare.

Migration outputs

Reports and a working Dynamics 365 CE destination.

Reports

  • Pre-migration customisation assessment report
  • Salesforce-to-Dynamics remove-versus-replace report
  • Design, architecture and migration estimates
  • Post-migration final report

Platform outcomes

  • Migrated Dynamics 365 CE with required Salesforce customisations and data
  • Copilot extensions for the migrated Dynamics 365 CE environment
  • Hypercare and post-migration optimisation
