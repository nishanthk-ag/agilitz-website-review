Features

Capability without unnecessary friction.

  • Centralised Image Handling: One place for CRM visuals tied to the business record.
  • Visual Context: Give service and operations teams the images they need to make faster decisions.
  • AI-Powered Discovery: Use AI tagging and search to retrieve relevant images quickly.
  • Secure & Scalable: Use Azure-backed storage and native Dynamics 365 integration.
