Home › About Agilitz

Strategists, design thinkers and engineers helping enterprises transform with Microsoft.

Agilitz is a Microsoft-focused technology services company specialising in Business Applications, Data & AI and transformation services. We help organisations modernise operations, improve customer engagement, create real-time insight and adopt intelligent automation with a practical, engineering-led approach.

Our vision

Build intelligent solutions that help enterprises transform, adapt and grow.

We connect business understanding, Microsoft platform expertise, data and intelligent automation to improve performance and create lasting value.

Our mission

“To build a valued global company which helps enterprises get a competitive edge by leveraging our expertise in Microsoft platforms.”

What makes Agilitz an ideal partner

Microsoft depth, business context and lifecycle ownership in one team.

Agilitz combines advisory, functional expertise, engineering, Data & AI and managed services so customers can move from a focused problem to a sustainable operating capability without repeatedly changing partners.

01

Cross-stack Microsoft capability

Dynamics 365 Finance, SCM, Commerce, Project Operations, Business Central, CE, Power Platform, Microsoft Fabric and AI can be addressed as one transformation landscape rather than isolated technologies.

02

Assurance before unnecessary build

Business process audits, solution assessments, cross-module reviews, data studies and technical assurance help identify the real constraint before implementation accelerates.

03

Full lifecycle delivery

Engage Agilitz for advisory, implementation, testing, migration, optimisation, hypercare or managed services — with continuity across the lifecycle.

04

Engineering plus reusable IP

Products, accelerators and engineering patterns extend Microsoft capability where needed while reducing avoidable custom build and delivery effort.

05

Operate after go-live

L1–L4 support, incident and problem management, ALM, CoE/GCC enablement and continuous improvement keep the platform moving after implementation.
