Enabling businesses for the digital era, through the power of people.

Agilitz brings strategists, design thinkers and engineers together around Microsoft Business Applications, Data & AI and transformation services.

Our mission

To build a valued global company which helps enterprises get a competitive edge by leveraging our expertise in Microsoft platforms.

Agilitz specialises in Microsoft Business Applications — Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations, Business Central, Customer Engagement, Power Platform and Data & AI — supporting organisations across Retail, Manufacturing, Consumer Goods and Professional Services.

Our focus is practical transformation: improving operational efficiency, strengthening customer engagement, creating real-time insight and applying intelligent automation where it creates measurable value.

  • Specialist Microsoft Business Applications capability
  • Deep Dynamics and data experience at leadership level
  • Flexible engagement models built around customer priorities
  • Proprietary products, frameworks and accelerators
  • Open, collaborative and predictable delivery
